The detail you skipped is the one that sinks the site

Leave no stone unturned

A client of ours did everything right. They found a site with enough power capacity. They pulled utilization data on nearby stations. Traffic, amenities, zoning, all of it checked out. Every box ticked. They signed the contract, ordered the hardware, and finished pouring concrete.

Then came the commissioning.

The chargers wouldn't connect to the server. They tried every provider. They discovered that the project is in a dead zone.

One thing they didn't check, and it cost them the project.

That's why we built the cellular signal strength map in EVpin. You can see coverage down to the block level before you commit. Dead zones show up instantly. One more stone turned over, before it's too late.
